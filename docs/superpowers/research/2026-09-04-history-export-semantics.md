# Streaming history semantics for the Crate views (2026-09-04)

Research behind the Crate design spec (`../specs/2026-09-04-crate-history-views-design.md`): what each `reason_end` value means, how community tools compute skip rates, timezone handling for month buckets, storage sizing for per-month counts, and threshold defaults. One research agent with web access; sources linked per fact.

## Recommendation

CLASSIFICATION. Keep reason_end as the primary signal and use ms_played only as a gate. Per record with a track URI: finished = reason_end === 'trackdone'; skipped = skipped === true OR reason_end in {'fwdbtn','backbtn','endplay','unknown'} (Ortham's rule, corroborated by the second export's cross-tab where fwdbtn/endplay/backbtn are 100% skipped=true and trackdone/logout/exits/remote are 100% false); everything else is neutral and excluded from the rate: 'logout', 'remote', 'trackerror', 'unexpected-exit', 'unexpected-exit-while-paused', 'switched-to-audio', null/'' and the pre-2017 leftovers 'appload','clickrow','clickside','playbtn','popup','uriopen' (tiny counts, folded into endplay since 2017). Do not trust the skipped flag alone (false for every record 2015-04-13..2022-10-16, null in older exports); use it only as an OR. Finish-rate view: rate = finished / (finished + skipped), computed over ALL track records regardless of ms_played (skips average ~14 s, so the 30 s credited-play filter would erase most of the denominator); keep the existing 30 s rule for 'plays'. Store per track: plays (>=30 s, as today), attempts (all track records incl. short, for tooltips), finished, skipped. Show the view only for tracks with finished+skipped >= 10 (n=10 gives roughly +/-30 pp at 95%; below that the rate is noise), two Segmented tabs 'Finished' (rate desc, tie-break plays) and 'Skipped' (rate asc), badge bands <=35% / 35-65% / >=65% finished. Skip the ms_played/duration ratio for now: 95% of trackdone plays already equal the API duration, outliers overshoot by minutes, and the export has no length; if a length is ever needed use TrackRow.durationMs from playlist sync when present, else the median (not max) of ms_played over that track's trackdone plays. MONTH BUCKETING. Bucket by ts (stream end) converted with the device's current IANA zone at import (new Date(ts).getFullYear()/getMonth()), which applies DST rules per instant automatically; store the zone name in meta and show it on the Import screen so a later device mismatch is visible; do not use offline_timestamp. Reasons: Spotify's own reporting is UTC-day but every user-facing tool that lets you pick (your_spotify) applies a local zone at read time, and a DJ in Europe listens late at night, so UTC would push post-22:00 plays on the last day of a month into the wrong month; travel and the end-vs-start-time question shift only plays within hours of a month edge (well under 1% of plays) and are not worth complexity. STORAGE SHAPE. Extend PlayRow (bump DB_VERSION to 2, rebuild the plays store from the next import rather than migrating) with months: Record<string, number> keyed 'YYYY-MM' -> credited plays, plus finished, skipped, attempts. Per year is NOT enough (the drill-down needs months) and storing years separately is redundant: years are a fold over months, computed once at load and cached in memory. Sizing for 20k tracks / 250k plays: ~77k month cells, ~1.8 MB serialized in IndexedDB, ~7 MB heap as nested objects (5 MB if packed as [monthIndex, count, ...] number arrays, only worth it if profiling shows pressure on Android). Keep msPlayed lifetime only; do not store ms per month. VIEW RULES. (1) Top by year: rank by plays in the selected year, tie-break msPlayed; year chips from range.first..range.last; optional month chips (All, Jan..Dec) filtering months[`${year}-${mm}`]; 'summer 2022' = tap Jun/Jul/Aug in a multi-select month chip row or keep single-month if simplicity wins. (2) Forgotten gems: plays >= 10 lifetime AND lastTs older than the window (Segmented 6 / 12 / 24 months, default 12 months; Spotify's Repeat Rewind uses 'over a month ago' but that is far too short for a rediscovery list); rank by plays desc; badge 'last played <Mon YYYY>'. (3) Heavy rotation: window Segmented 1 / 3 / 6 months (default 3; Spotify's On Repeat uses 30 days), rank by plays in window (sum of months), require >= 3 plays in window; show 'x of y lifetime' and a 'new' badge when plays outside the window == 0. (4) All-time classics: yearsActive = count of years with >= 3 plays (3 filters out single-listen years without hiding tracks played only a few times per year), require yearsActive >= 3, rank by yearsActive desc then plays desc; badge 'N of M years' with M = export span. (5) Finish rate as above. All five are pure functions over PlayRow[] held in memory (20k rows, one pass each, sub-100 ms on a phone), so no extra indexes or workers are needed; one 'History' tab holding a Segmented(Top by year / Forgotten / Heavy rotation / Classics / Finish rate) keeps the five-slot bottom bar intact.

## Facts

- **[verified-secondary] reason_end meanings (Spotify ReadMeFirst text, transcribed).** A Portuguese-language analysis reproduces Spotify's field descriptions in English (with PDF copy-paste artifacts such as 'theuser', 'startedon', 'buttonor'): reason_end 'trackdone' = "The track played to its end"; 'fwdbtn' = "The user pressed Next/Forward"; 'endplay' = "The user started playing something else (by clicking a track or Play/Shuffle button or similar)"; 'backbtn' = "The user pressed Back"; 'logout' = "The app was shut down or the user logged out."; 'remote' = "The track playback was moved to another device"; 'unexpected-exit' = "The app was shut down either by the user, or by the operating system, or it crashed."; 'unexpected-exit-while-paused' = "The playback was paused, and at some point after that, the app was shut down either by the user, or by the operating system, or it crashed."; 'trackerror' = "The playback of the previous track ran into some form of error..."; 'clickrow' = "The user pressed a specific track in a list of tracks."; 'unknown' = "The reason why the user (or the app) started playing this track is unknown". The same page quotes ts as "Date and time of when the stream ended in UTC format (Coordinated Universal Time zone)". No descriptions are given for clickside, popup, uriopen, appload, playbtn, switched-to-audio or the empty string.
  Source: https://rstudio-pubs-static.s3.amazonaws.com/1044086_ea8846260e194b659b473ee5d5c51010.html
- **[verified-secondary] reason_end values seen in real exports and obsolete ones.** Ortham (227k streams, 2014-2024): reason_end has values appload, backbtn, clickrow, clickside, endplay, fwdbtn, logout, playbtn, popup, remote, trackdone, trackerror, unexpected-exit, unexpected-exit-while-paused, unknown, uriopen and an empty string. "appload (202 counts), clickrow (344), clickside (28), playbtn (3), popup (209), uriopen (11) and the empty string value (19) seem to have been obsoleted during my break from Spotify between 2015 and 2017... My guess is that they were folded into endplay". reason_start additionally shows 'switched-to-audio' in 2025 exports (music-video to audio switch); it is not documented anywhere.
  Source: https://blog.ortham.net/posts/2024-12-21-spotify-streaming-history-part-1/
- **[verified-secondary] skipped boolean reliability and skip rule.** Ortham: "all values between 2015-04-13 and 2022-10-16 are false, with a mix of true and false values appearing on either side of that time range." Correlation with reliable periods: backbtn 866 true/2835 false, endplay 2692/3979, fwdbtn 34526/97952, unknown 17/6; "backbtn and unknown ... only appear with skipped: true otherwise"; "endplay and fwdbtn are not present with skipped: false after 2022-10-16". Conclusion: "the check for if a stream was skipped should be skipped or reason_end in ('backbtn', 'unknown', 'endplay', 'fwdbtn')". Part 5 counts 'Complete streams' as reason_end = 'trackdone' and excludes ('', appload, clickrow, playbtn, popup, uriopen, unknown) as noise in the reason_end-over-time chart.
  Source: https://blog.ortham.net/posts/2025-01-12-spotify-streaming-history-part-5/
- **[verified-secondary] skipped x reason_end cross-tab in a second real export.** In a 177k-record export where skipped is mostly NA, the non-NA rows show: trackdone 5014 false / 0 true; fwdbtn 0 false / 2154 true; endplay 0 / 517 true; backbtn 0 / 221 true; logout 337 false / 0; unexpected-exit-while-paused 18 false; unexpected-exit 14 false; remote 13 false; empty string 0 false / 26 true; unknown and trackerror only NA. Confirms fwdbtn/endplay/backbtn = skipped, trackdone/logout/exit/remote = not skipped.
  Source: https://rstudio-pubs-static.s3.amazonaws.com/1044086_ea8846260e194b659b473ee5d5c51010.html
- **[verified-secondary] How community tools treat skips.** your_spotify's extended-history importer reads only ts, ms_played, spotify_track_uri and the two name fields, drops records with ms_played < 30*1000 ("If track was played for less than 30 seconds"), and never uses reason_end or skipped; played_at = ts unchanged.
  Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Yooooomi/your_spotify/master/apps/server/src/tools/importers/full_privacy.ts
- **[verified-secondary] How community tools treat skips.** stats.fm import docs: excluded from stats are "Skipped tracks* (since they make your stats inaccurate)", "Streams shorter than 30 seconds* (since they make your stats inaccurate)" and local files/podcasts; "These types of streams are saved in the database, but 'hidden' from the stats." stats.fm publishes no skip-rate formula or minimum sample; its FAQ only says "for stats.fm to track your stream properly, you need to fully listen to it".
  Source: https://support.stats.fm/docs/import/
- **[verified-secondary] How community tools treat skips.** spotify-unzipped (browser tool, 2026) passes the raw flag through (`skipped: raw.skipped ?? false`), stores reason_end untouched, computes no skip rate, and buckets dates in UTC by string slicing: `dateKey: raw.ts.slice(0, 10)`, `hour: parseInt(raw.ts.slice(11, 13), 10)`; aggregations use `dateKey.slice(0, 7)` for months with TOP_N = 25 and no minimum play count.
  Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NateShoffner/spotify-unzipped/master/src/utils/recordClassifier.ts
- **[verified-secondary] How community tools treat skips.** piebro/spotify-statistics defines `df["full_play"] = df["reason_end"] == "trackdone"`, drops the skipped column, applies no ms_played filter and no timezone conversion, and replaces ts with offline_timestamp when offline_timestamp > 100; its README notes that for 2017 "reason_end data field consistently showed none instead of the actual reason".
  Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/piebro/spotify-statistics/main/src/create_db.py
- **[verified-secondary] How community tools treat skips.** playbackstats.com: "The dashboard uses a transparent 30-second threshold for its qualified-play rankings" while "Total listening hours still include every music listening event"; with extended fields it compares "skips, shuffle listening, offline playback, and tracks played to their natural end" (i.e. trackdone), no formula or minimum published.
  Source: https://playbackstats.com/spotify
- **[verified-secondary] 30-second play rule.** ListenBrainz import guide filters `ms_played >= 30000` ("we are working with a 30 seconds time for this to match the defaults") and drops records with null track or artist name; Spotify's own rule is a stream counts after 30 seconds and Wrapped counts "a play ... once you've listened for more than 30 seconds".
  Source: https://www.marcolussetti.com/misc/2025/07/03/listenbrainz-extended-history-import.html
- **[verified-secondary] ms_played vs track length as a skip signal.** Ortham joined the export to Web API duration_ms: of 18,679,692,394 ms of trackdone streams, 980,322,737 ms (~5%) had ms_played != duration_ms (552,735,720 ms with duration_ms > ms_played, 427,587,017 ms with duration_ms < ms_played). Where ms_played == duration_ms exactly, reason_end was trackdone 74,837 times, endplay 348, fwdbtn 9 (plus logout). "my mean time before skipping a track is 13.8 seconds"; "the mean length of a complete stream is 3.95 minutes" vs "the mean length of a stream is 1.56 minutes".
  Source: https://blog.ortham.net/posts/2025-01-12-spotify-streaming-history-part-5/
- **[verified-secondary] ms_played can exceed track duration.** Spotify Community thread 'msPlayed is longer than track.duration_ms': in most cases the two differ by a few milliseconds (loading time), but outliers reach 632,948 ms (~14 minutes) over the track length; a suggested cause is restarting the song before it finished. No Spotify staff explanation. (Page returns 403 to fetchers; content taken from search index.)
  Source: https://community.spotify.com/t5/Spotify-for-Developers/msPlayed-is-londer-than-track-duration-ms/td-p/5340621
- **[verified-official] Timezone: what ts is.** Spotify's official field list defines the timestamp as the date/time "of when the stream ended in UTC format"; real values are ISO-8601 with 'Z' and second precision. Ortham found 65,946 streams overlapping their predecessor and estimates "about 8% of ts values ... potentially being wrong".
  Source: https://support.spotify.com/us/article/understanding-my-data/
- **[verified-official] Timezone: what Spotify itself does.** Spotify for Artists: stats use Coordinated Universal Time; "A streaming day runs from 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM UTC, no matter which country the stream happens in" and the zone cannot be changed. The Wrapped methodology post (2025-12-05) states the >30 s play rule and the January-to-mid-November window but says nothing about time zones.
  Source: https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/time-zone-for-stats-in-spotify-for-artists/
- **[verified-secondary] Timezone: what community tools do.** your_spotify stores played_at in UTC and applies an IANA zone at query time: README `TIMEZONE | Europe/Paris | The timezone of your stats, only affects read requests since data is saved with UTC time`; "Any user can set his proper timezone in the settings, it will be used for any computed statistics." statsTools.ts builds year/month/day/week/hour with MongoDB `$year/$month/... { date: "$played_at", timezone: userTimezone ?? TIMEZONE }`.
  Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Yooooomi/your_spotify/master/apps/server/src/database/queries/statsTools.ts
- **[verified-secondary] Timezone: what community tools do.** spotify-unzipped, piebro/spotify-statistics, Ortham (Postgres extract(hour from timestamp)) and jhrcook (lubridate ymd_hm on the UTC string) all bucket by the UTC value without conversion; only your_spotify converts to a user zone. Bucketing by end time (ts) is universal; no tool subtracts ms_played to bucket by start time.
  Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NateShoffner/spotify-unzipped/master/src/utils/recordClassifier.ts
- **[verified-secondary] Thresholds: Spotify's own recency playlists.** Spotify's On Repeat "stays up to date with the songs you've had on loop the most over the last 30 days"; Repeat Rewind holds "songs you frequently played back in the day (well, over a month ago)" and "will update every five days"; a track never appears in both.
  Source: https://www.engadget.com/2019-09-24-spotify-personalized-playlists-current-obsessions.html
- **[verified-secondary] Thresholds: forgotten favourites.** Spotify's Prompted Playlist launch (2026-01-28) gives as an official example "songs I've saved to my Library ... that I either haven't played yet or have only listened to once"; Platformer's author tested his own prompt "songs that I have listened to at least 20 times but haven't streamed in the past two months" (user-devised, not a Spotify rule). No community tool publishes a 'forgotten gems' or 'classics' threshold; Ortham's 'most skipped tracks' chart is a plain top-10 by skipped count with no minimum sample.
  Source: https://www.platformer.news/spotify-prompted-playlists-ai-meta-vibes/
- **[unverified] Thresholds: skip-rate bands.** Marketing sources categorise Spotify skip rate as low (<20%), moderate (20-40%) and high (>40%); no minimum play count is stated. Treat as folklore, useful only as badge bands.
  Source: https://www.chartlex.com/blog/streaming/spotify-skip-rate-how-to-reduce-2026

## Open questions

- Spotify's actual ReadMeFirst PDF was not fetched; the value descriptions come from one analysis that transcribed it (with PDF spacing artifacts). Meanings of clickside, popup, uriopen, appload, playbtn and switched-to-audio as reason_end remain undocumented.
- Which time zone Spotify Wrapped uses for its year boundary is not stated anywhere official; only Spotify for Artists documents UTC days.
- stats.fm hides 'skipped' streams but never says whether that is the skipped flag, reason_end, or a duration ratio; no tool publishes a minimum sample size for a skip/finish rate.
- Why ms_played can exceed the track length by minutes (restart, repeat-one, crossfade, pause accounting?) is unexplained; it makes max(ms_played) a poor length estimator versus median or TrackRow.durationMs.
- Whether the owner's export contains the 2017 null/empty reason_end gap and null skipped values (both reported by others); the neutral bucket handles it but the finish-rate view may be empty for those years.
- Counting the finish rate over all attempts (including sub-30 s records) requires changing PlayAggregator, which currently drops 'short' records before aggregation.
- Whether plays in Streaming_History_Video_*.json (music videos with track URIs) should feed these views; the current importer's isHistoryFile decides that.
- Device zone at import time versus historical location: conn_country could flag travel periods, but no tool does this and it was not evaluated.

## Design panel

Three designs (minimalist, DJ-workflow, timeline explorer) were scored by two judges; "The Crate" (DJ-workflow) won both and the synthesis grafted the judges' suggestions. The synthesis is the basis of the spec.
